# ⚖️ Know Your Rights

### AI-Powered Legal Awareness & Guidance Platform

Know Your Rights is an AI-powered legal awareness platform designed to help users understand their legal concerns, discover relevant laws, and identify practical next steps.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [Visit Know Your Rights](https://adarsh007-code.github.io/Know-Your-Rights/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **AI-Powered Legal Guidance** — Get an initial overview of legal concerns and possible next steps.
- **Legal Information Discovery** — Explore relevant laws and legal topics.
- **Case Analysis** — Organize information related to a legal issue.
- **Evidence Checklist** — Identify potentially relevant documents and evidence.
- **Actionable First Steps** — Understand practical actions to consider.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Access legal information through a straightforward interface.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- React

- AI integration used by the project

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of Know Your Rights is to make legal information easier to understand and help people identify useful starting points when facing legal concerns.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Know Your Rights is an informational tool and does not replace advice from a qualified legal professional. AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate. Verify applicable laws and consult a qualified professional before making important legal decisions.
